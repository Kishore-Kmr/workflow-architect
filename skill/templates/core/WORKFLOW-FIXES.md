# Workflow fixes

Every fix to this workflow, newest at the bottom: why (evidence from LOG.md), what changed, its
size, who made it, what should improve, when it is re-checked, how to undo it, and the outcome.
Written with `tools/wf.py fix add` and moved on with `tools/wf.py fix mark` (see
`tools/fixes.md`). Progress review reports are added here too.
