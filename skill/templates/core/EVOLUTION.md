# Evolution

Every change to this workflow, newest at the bottom: why (evidence from LOG.md), what changed,
its size, who applied it, what should improve, when it is checked again, how to undo it, and
the outcome. Written with `tools/wf.py change add` and moved on with `tools/wf.py change mark`
(see `tools/evolve.md`). Look-back reports (`L-<n>`) are added here too.
